About

Practical calculators, explained clearly.

Calculator Readiness is an independent collection of browser-based tools designed to make everyday calculations easier to understand and verify.

What we value

Clarity

Each focused calculator explains its formula, assumptions, and limitations alongside the result.

Privacy

Calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to a server for calculation.

Transparency

Methods are documented, pages carry review dates, and estimates are labeled when real-world factors can change the outcome.

Corrections: If you find a calculation or content issue, report it through the project repository. Security reports should use the private channel listed in our security.txt.

What these tools are not

Calculator Readiness provides general informational estimates. It does not provide medical, legal, investment, lending, tax, or other professional advice. Important decisions should include current terms and guidance from an appropriately qualified professional.
