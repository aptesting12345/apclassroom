How the split is calculated

Tip = bill × tip percentage
Total = bill + tip
Per-person share = total ÷ number of people

Example

An $84.50 bill with a 20% tip produces a $16.90 tip and a $101.40 total. Split equally among three people, each share is $33.80.

Before splitting

This tool divides the full bill equally. If people ordered different amounts, calculate individual subtotals first or agree on how shared items should be handled.
