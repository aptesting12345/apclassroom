Percentage formulas

X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y
Percentage change = (new − original) ÷ |original| × 100
Part as a percent of whole = part ÷ whole × 100

Example

A price rising from $80 to $100 increased by $20. Divide $20 by the original $80 and multiply by 100: the increase is 25%.

A common mistake

Percentage change uses the original value as the denominator. Reversing the starting and ending values produces a different percentage because it answers a different question.
