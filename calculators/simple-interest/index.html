Simple interest formula

Simple interest applies the rate to the original principal for the entire term. Previously earned interest does not earn additional interest.

Interest = principal × annual rate × time

Example

At 5% simple interest, $5,000 earns $250 per year. Over three years, the interest is $750 and the final balance is $5,750.

Simple versus compound interest

Simple interest grows by the same dollar amount each period. Compound interest recalculates returns using a growing balance, so it usually produces a different result over longer periods.
